About Me

I am a computational social scientist and PhD student in Political Science at the University of Florida, with a minor in Statistical Computing. My research focuses on understanding the spatial and temporal dynamics of political violence, ethnic conflict, and communal war.

As a Data Scientist at the Violence, Conflict, and Security Lab (ViCS), I build predictive models to evaluate competing theories of political violence onset worldwide. My work combines conventional statistical methods with modern machine learning and AI techniques to uncover patterns in human behavior and explain the motivations behind those patterns.

My dissertation, "Explaining the Spatial Logic of Ethnic Violence," examines why communal violence emerges and peaks in some localities but not others, with a particular focus on Iraq (2003-2009).
