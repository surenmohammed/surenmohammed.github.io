Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy centers on bridging theory and practice. I believe students learn best when they can connect abstract concepts to real-world problems. In my courses, I emphasize:

  • Active learning: Engaging students through discussions, case studies, and hands-on exercises
  • Methodological rigor: Teaching students to think critically about evidence and causation
  • Real-world relevance: Connecting course material to current events and policy debates
  • Inclusive environment: Creating a classroom where diverse perspectives are valued
